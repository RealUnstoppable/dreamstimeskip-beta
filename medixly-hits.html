Founder & Lead Developer of Medixly. Audio software engineer researching browser-based digital signal processing, spatial audio, and music recommendation systems.

Every single day, hundreds of thousands of new audio clips and songs are uploaded across TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and streaming platforms. Out of that deafening avalanche of sound, only a handful of tracks catch fire and lodge themselves into the collective consciousness of millions of listeners.

At Medixly (formerly HarmonyTunes), our mission has always been simple: eliminate the friction between hearing a viral track in a short video clip and enjoying its full high-fidelity studio version. But behind our clean, neon-lit web interface lies a sophisticated blend of trend analytics, browser-based digital signal processing (DSP), and hands-on human musical curation.

"Algorithmic recommendations on major platforms tend to trap listeners in echo chambers—serving the same thirty mainstream songs on repeat. Medixly was designed to do the exact opposite: detect authentic cultural momentum the moment it sparks in grassroots creator communities."

1. The Shift in Modern Music Discovery

Ten years ago, music discovery was dictated by radio gatekeepers and corporate editorial playlists. Today, cultural velocity is bottom-up. A bedroom producer in London or a teenager in Tokyo can drop a 15-second snippet with a hypnotic bassline, and within forty-eight hours, half a million creators have used it as background audio for their videos.

However, major streaming services are remarkably slow to adapt to this dynamic. Listeners frequently find themselves scouring comment sections asking "What's the song in this clip?" only to discover the track is unreleased, miscredited, or buried under dozens of pitch-shifted impostor uploads. Medixly solves this problem by directly bridging the gap between social audio trends and instant web playback.

2. The Hybrid Curation Engine: Data Meets Human Taste

Computers are exceptional at identifying numerical growth curves, but they have zero innate understanding of rhythm, emotional resonance, or cultural vibe. If you rely solely on algorithms, your music platform ends up filled with artificial spam and engagement-hacked soundbites.

Medixly operates on a strict Hybrid Curation Pipeline:

  1. Ingestion & Velocity Detection: Our automated discovery pipeline monitors public metadata feeds for sudden surges in creation velocity—identifying songs whose user-generated creations are multiplying exponentially over a rolling 72-hour window.
  2. Acoustic Fingerprinting & Match Verification: We cross-reference the viral audio snippets with authoritative databases (including Apple Music, Spotify, and MusicBrainz) to locate the legitimate full-length source master and ensure proper credit to original creators.
  3. Human Curation & Mastering Check: Every single candidate track is manually auditioned by our editorial team. We test for mix quality, dynamic range, and replay value before any track is approved for our front-page rotation.

3. Web Audio Architecture: Bringing Studio Control to the Browser

Most web audio players treat sound as an afterthought, relying on standard HTML5 <audio> tags that offer little more than basic volume sliders. At Medixly, we built our audio player on top of the native Web Audio API graph.

By routing the media stream through a custom chain of BiquadFilterNode instances, Medixly provides listeners with genuine real-time audio shaping directly in the browser:

  • 10-Band Graphic Equalizer: Users can tune frequency bands from 32 Hz sub-bass up to 16 kHz airy highs, tailored specifically for headphones, car audio systems, or studio monitors.
  • Dynamic Compression & Limiting: A built-in DynamicsCompressorNode prevents ear fatigue and harsh clipping when transitioning between differently mastered community tracks.
  • Binaural Stereo Panning: Subtle psychoacoustic widening gives standard stereo mixes a spacious, immersive soundstage that feels open and fatigue-free during long study or gaming sessions.

4. Supporting Independent Artists & Ethical Attribution

One of the biggest tragedies of the short-form video era is that independent musicians frequently get stripped of their attribution. When their audio becomes a trending "sound," millions of people hear their craft without ever knowing their name or where to find their full discography.

Medixly takes attribution seriously. Every track on our platform includes prominent links to the original artist's official streaming profiles, social channels, and merchandise stores. We do not pirate or monetize artist master recordings; rather, our player serves as an interactive discovery gateway that channels engaged fans directly back to the creators who made the music.

5. The Road Ahead: AI Stems, Offline Caching, and Mobile Suite

We are constantly expanding what is possible within a lightweight web environment. Over the next year, our development roadmap includes several exciting milestones:

  • Client-Side Stem Separation: Exploring WebAssembly (Wasm) and WebGPU models to allow listeners to isolate vocals, basslines, or drums on the fly directly inside their browser without cloud latency.
  • Persistent Offline Cache (IndexedDB): Enabling instant offline playback for favorite playlists via service worker caching and encrypted IndexedDB storage, ideal for flights or poor cellular reception.
  • Native Mobile Companion: Bringing Medixly's viral trend radar to iOS and Android with background audio support and widget integrations.
