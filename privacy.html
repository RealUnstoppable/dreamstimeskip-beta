1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide to us, as well as certain data automatically captured when you navigate our platforms:

  • Automated Device & Usage Information: We may collect browser type, operating system version, page access timestamps, and referral URLs to ensure performance and prevent abuse.

4. Automated Telemetry, Behavioral Analytics, and Commercial Data Monetization

To deliver high-performance user experiences, diagnose user friction, optimize conversion funnels, and power first-party attribution modeling for https://realunstoppable.store, we deploy advanced first-party client-side telemetry collectors and server-side ingestion pipelines backed by Google Cloud Firestore.

4.1 Scope of Telemetry & Micro-Interaction Signals

When you browse our storefront and web applications, our first-party collectors systematically record granular engagement metrics, including:

  • Micro-Interactions & Friction Diagnostics: Scroll depth thresholds (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%), cumulative visible dwell duration, call-to-action (CTA) hover durations (>400ms), rage clicks (rapid successive clicks within a confined pixel radius indicative of broken UX), dead clicks (clicks yielding no DOM mutation or state update within 500ms), and promotional code copy/paste activities.
  • Catalog & Commerce Telemetry: Product SKU impressions (observed via viewport intersection with dwell >1000ms), cart mutations, checkout step completions, and purchase confirmations.
  • Cross-Browser Technical Environment: Client screen dimensions, device pixel ratios, viewport metrics, color depth, hardware concurrency, device memory hints, client time zone, browser language preferences, effective network connection types, and high-entropy Client Hints where permitted.

4.2 Strict Privacy Guardrails & Exclusion of Sensitive Inputs

Our telemetry collectors operate under strict data hygiene rules: we strictly prohibit and never capture the raw values of passwords (<input type="password">), payment card numbers, card expiration dates, card verification codes (CVV/CVC), Social Security Numbers (SSN), or sensitive personal communications. On non-sensitive form inputs, we only log field focus timing, duration, and boolean completion/abandonment indicators.

4.3 Pseudonymization, Cryptographic Hashing & Google Ads Attribution

All direct personally identifiable information (PII)—including email addresses and telephone numbers captured during checkout or newsletter signups—is sanitized, lowercased, and cryptographically hashed using industry-standard SHA-256 before any transmission or storage in our telemetry ledger. We leverage first-party identifiers (ru_uid and rolling session ru_sid) and HTTP-only cookies to enable Google Consent Mode v2 and Google Ads Enhanced Conversions without relying on third-party tracking cookies.

4.4 Commercial Data Monetization, Cohort Licensing & State Privacy Opt-Outs

We analyze telemetry patterns to synthesize aggregated, pseudonymous behavioral audience cohorts (such as high_intent_viewer, cart_abandoner, repeat_buyer, and promo_seeker). Subject to applicable legal authorizations and your affirmative consent choices, such pseudonymous audience cohorts may be shared, licensed, or utilized in commercial advertising syndication and Google Ads Customer Match campaigns.

Your Privacy Choices & Global Privacy Control (GPC): In strict compliance with the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100 et seq.), Colorado Privacy Act (CPA), Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA), and other comprehensive state privacy statutes, you possess the absolute right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising. Our platform automatically recognizes and honors the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal (transmitted via browser header Sec-GPC: 1 or JavaScript property navigator.globalPrivacyControl === true). When GPC is active or when you select "Do Not Sell or Share" in our privacy modal, commercial data sharing and targeted advertising are instantly revoked and locked to denied across our systems.

You can adjust your granular preferences or exercise your opt-out rights at any time by clicking the button in our website footer.
