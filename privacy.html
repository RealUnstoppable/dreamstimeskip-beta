1. Information We Collect

We collect information to deliver seamless services, operate player experiences, process transactions, and communicate updates to you. The categories of data collected include:

  • Account Credentials: Email address, username, display preferences, password hashes, and membership status when you register an account.
  • Order & Billing Details: Name, shipping address, order history, and loyalty point totals when purchasing from the Unstoppable Shop. Payment card numbers are processed securely by our certified PCI-compliant gateway (Stripe) and are not stored on our servers.
  • Usage & Interaction Data: Streamed tracks, audio player volume/repeat preferences, playlist configurations, and blog comments.
  • Device & Network Data: IP address, browser type, operating system version, referrer URL, and basic device diagnostics collected automatically via server logs and Google AdSense tags.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information strictly for legitimate business and operational purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and enhance the Unstoppable Hub, Medixly Music, and Dreams TimeSkip portals.
  • To authenticate user sessions, manage subscriptions, and grant access to Premium/Ultimate features.
  • To fulfill orders, deliver merchandise, track shipments, and award loyalty points.
  • To communicate platform updates, release announcements, and respond to support inquiries.
  • To protect our users, detect fraud, enforce our Terms and Conditions, and maintain site security.

3. Cookies & Advertising Technologies

We utilize cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to remember your preferences (such as selected color themes, active volume levels, and session state) and deliver relevant content.

Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve advertisements based on your prior visits to our Site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our platform and other sites across the internet.

Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's My Ad Center. Additionally, Premium and Ultimate subscribers are granted an ad-free viewing experience across the Unstoppable ecosystem.

Cookie Consent Banner

Our cookie banner prompts you upon your first visit to accept or decline optional cookies. By accepting or continuing to use the platform, you acknowledge our use of cookies and consent to our Terms and Conditions and this Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Partners & Services

  • Stripe: Secure payment processing for merchandise and recurring memberships.
  • Google AdSense: Monetization and advertising delivery for free-tier users.
  • Discord & Social Platforms: Community channels and social links.
