## 2024-10-26 - Add ARIA labels to More Options buttons\n**Learning:** In dynamically generated lists with dropdown options, 'More Options' buttons represented by '...' icons lacked accessible names, preventing screen readers from identifying which item the options relate to.\n**Action:** Used aria-label with the dynamically scoped item title (e.g. `aria-label="More options for ${escapeHTML(group.baseTitle)}"`) to provide context-specific accessible names for icon-only buttons in lists.
