The Chronicles of the NeoCube

Centuries have passed since the first exiles set foot in the mysterious lands of time. They, the pioneers who forged the NeoCube, a sacred artifact capable of bending reality itself, unknowingly unleashed a force that would shape the fate of kingdoms across generations.

With its discovery, an enigmatic figure emerged, wielding the NeoCube's power to control these lands and dictate the rise and fall of mighty realms. Yet, amidst this dominion, a glimmer of resistance remains. Three kingdoms, resolute in their defiance, defy the oppressive rule:

🪙 The Kingdom of Wealth

Where opulence, gold trade, and thriving bazaars drive unyielding merchant dynasties.

⚔️ The Kingdom of Power

A bastion of military discipline, veteran warriors, and impregnable stone fortifications.

🔮 The Kingdom of Wisdom

Where arcane knowledge, alchemical mastery, and ancient scholarship illuminate the path ahead.

👑 The Vacant Throne

Noble bloodlines have splintered. Gather your loyal subjects and claim your birthright as Supreme King.

Patch Notes & Known Issues

We've addressed critical glitches including player model leg rendering and shield collision failures, alongside ongoing monitoring of celestial rendering bugs.

✓ Resolved Fixes

  • Model Rendering: Fixed issue where players spawned without legs.
  • Shield Mechanics: Fixed shields failing to block while sneaking.
  • Aerial Saturation: Corrected bug preventing hunger restoration when eating while flying.
  • Elytra Propulsion: Fixed jump button bug causing unintended launch into space.
  • Coordinate Glitch: Eliminated forced teleportation to coordinates -1287, 89, 324.
  • Inventory Protection: Fixed rare inventory loss bug upon consuming food.
  • Ender Pearls: No longer causes fatal impact damage on ocean surfaces.
  • NPC Spawns: NPCs properly restricted from crowding active farmland.

⚠ Known Bugs Being Monitored

  • Certain custom entities render with fallback missing texture grids.
  • Cows occasionally drop "Bucket of M!lk" instead of standard Milk.
  • The sun remains a deadly laser in specific high-altitude biomes.
  • The moon appears unusually enlarged during lunar transitions.
  • Elytra disengage trigger delay in creative testing mode.
  • Excessive wild flower clusters on high mountain slopes.

New Gear: Gilded Netherite & The Mace

A new pinnacle of equipment has arrived. Forge Gilded Netherite for supreme durability, +4 bonus health, and piglin pacification. Features the high-impact Mace weapon class!

3D In-Game Model — Gilded Netherite Armor (Designed by Catalin & Liz)

Crafting Process

  1. Smelt Blackstone: Smelt Gilded Blackstone in a standard or blast furnace to extract Melted Gilded Blackstone.
  2. Forge Ingot: Combine with Netherite to craft Gilded Netherite Ingots.
  3. Smithing Upgrade: Apply ingots to standard Netherite gear in a Smithing Table.

The Mace Weapon Class

The Mace shatters cobwebs and bamboo instantaneously. In combat, executing a Bash Attack (Right Click or Hold on mobile) inflicts catastrophic durability damage to opponent armor:

  • Gilded Netherite Mace: 50 durability damage per bash (2539 durability, 6 base attack damage).
  • Netherite Mace: 42 durability damage per bash.
  • Diamond Mace: 35 durability damage per bash.
  • Attack Range: ~2.9 blocks with a fast 0.5s attack cooldown.

Magic System & Elemental Wands

Wield 30 custom crafted wands across 10 elemental schools. Craft the Magic Book to progress through tiered masteries while maintaining balanced survival PvP.

Official Elemental Wands & The Magic Spellbook (Catalin's Magic Addon)

Elemental Schools

To ensure balanced factions, each adventurer may specialize in a maximum of two magic schools simultaneously. Craft the Magic Book to initiate your studies.

Elemental Affinities

  • 💨 Wind: Mobility boosts, repelling gusts, and glider acceleration.
  • 🌊 Water: Conduit healing, underwater agility, and tidal pushback.
  • 🔥 Fire: Thermal projectile bursts, lingering flames, and area blaze.
  • ⚡ Electric: Lightning strike summons and chain paralysis shock.
  • ❄️ Ice: Frost paths, slowing blizzards, and glacial shields.

Occult & Natural Affinities

  • 🌿 Grass: Root snares, thorn retaliation, and rapid botanical growth.
  • 🧪 Poison: Lingering decay clouds, hunger drain, and venom strikes.
  • ✨ Light: Healing auras, darkness dispelling, and flash blinding.
  • 💀 Necromancy: Minion reanimation, bone armor, and spectral sights.
  • ☠️ Death: High-risk life drain, wither infliction, and soul sacrifices.
